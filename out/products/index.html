Products

We don't just solve problems. We build what we wish existed.

At Ethora Labs, we believe useful products begin with a clear understanding of an everyday problem. We explore those problems, question existing workflows, and build thoughtful software that makes complex tasks simpler.

Some problems deserve more than a workaround.

  • Useful products often begin with small frustrations people encounter repeatedly.
  • A better solution does not always require a revolutionary technology.
  • Sometimes it requires understanding a workflow and removing unnecessary friction.
  • We value practical usefulness over feature count.
  • We aim to build products that are dependable, understandable and respectful of users.

The same engineering practices that shape our consulting work — code review, testing, honest trade-offs — shape what we choose to build and how we build it.

How we think about products

Observe. Question. Simplify. Build. Validate. Improve.

An illustrative framework for how an idea becomes something worth shipping — not a record that every product here has completed every stage.

  1. 1

    What is actually happening?

    Understand an actual problem and its context — who has it, how often, and what they do about it today.

  2. 2

    Why does it work this way?

    Challenge existing assumptions and workflows, including the ones that have simply never been questioned before.

  3. 3

    What is the smallest real fix?

    Identify the smallest useful solution — not the most impressive one, the one that actually removes the friction.

  4. 4

    Can this be trusted?

    Engineer a dependable implementation: the part that has to work correctly every time, not just in a demo.

  5. 5

    Does it actually work?

    Test whether it works technically and solves the intended problem — both questions, not just the first one.

  6. 6

    What did we learn?

    Refine based on evidence and feedback, and feed what is learned back into the next thing observed.

Developer Tools · Software Quality

Culprit

Every failure leaves a clue.

Culprit is a proprietary Chrome extension designed to help developers and software testers investigate browser failures.

Debugging becomes unnecessarily difficult when the context surrounding a failure is missing. Culprit aims to make that context easier to capture and communicate.

  • Capture of user actions
  • Selector generation
  • Network failure diagnostics
  • Screenshots
  • More actionable debugging information

Renewal & Recurring-Commitment Management

Recurr

Know before it renews.

Recurr helps businesses and individuals manage renewals and recurring commitments. It goes beyond reminders by helping users make decisions, track real-world actions, and confirm outcomes.

  1. Add
  2. Remind
  3. Decide
  4. Act
  5. Confirm
  6. Protect Savings

Renewal dates are easy to overlook, but the larger problem is that decisions and actions often happen too late. Recurr is designed to make upcoming commitments visible and help users act before deadlines.

Business

  • SaaS subscriptions
  • Vendors and suppliers
  • Leases
  • Insurance
  • Service contracts
  • Maintenance agreements

Personal

  • Memberships
  • Subscriptions
  • Domains
  • Warranties
  • Insurance

Worth knowing

Recurr does not automatically cancel subscriptions, negotiate contracts, or guarantee savings. Choosing Cancel records a decision, not a completed cancellation. Potential savings are not realised savings — they become Protected only once the real-world outcome is confirmed.

Different problems. The same principles.

Culprit and Recurr solve very different problems, for very different people, and they do not share a platform, data or infrastructure. What connects them is how we approach building anything:

  • Reduce unnecessary complexity.
  • Make important information visible.
  • Help people act with greater confidence.
  • Respect user control.
  • Build dependable, practical tools.

We build products because we believe engineering should make everyday work clearer, simpler and more dependable. Culprit and Recurr are two expressions of that belief — and our curiosity doesn't end there.
