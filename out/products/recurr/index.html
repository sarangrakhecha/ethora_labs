Inside Recurr

A closer look at how Recurr tracks commitments, highlights decisions, and measures savings.

Visit recurr.fyi

  • Overview: what needs a decision, surfaced first — not buried in a list.

  • Renewals: ordered by the last day you can still change your mind.

  • Vendors: every recurring commitment in one directory, not scattered across inboxes.

  • Savings: a figure only counts once the real-world action is actually confirmed.

What it does

  • A clear daily overview

    What needs a decision, what's renewing soon, and savings identified versus actually protected — in one view.

  • Renewals, ordered by urgency

    Sorted by the last date you can still act, with a visible countdown to that deadline rather than just the renewal date itself.

  • Every vendor in one place

    Software, suppliers, leases and service contracts together, each with its category, owner and annual cost.

  • Savings you can trust

    Verified savings, avoided spend, and cost opportunity are tracked separately, so a potential saving is never shown as money already saved.

Why Recurr exists

The problem was never really remembering a renewal date. Calendars already do that. The real problem is that recurring financial and contractual commitments can renew before anyone has consciously decided what to do about them.

Recurr aims to turn renewal information into timely, actionable decisions — made before the deadline, not discovered after it.

Product vision

Forward it. We'll track it.

Automation by default. Intervention by exception.

How Recurr is designed to turn a forwarded email into a tracked renewal — not a claim that every step runs in production today.

  1. 1Forward a renewal, invoice, subscription or contract email to track@recurr.fyi
  2. 2Recurr identifies the approved sender and which workspace it belongs to
  3. 3It extracts the relevant renewal information from the email
  4. 4High-confidence renewals are added automatically
  5. 5Ambiguous information is sent back for a targeted, quick review
  6. 6Reminders are scheduled around the dates that actually require a decision
  7. 7You decide: Keep, Reduce, Cancel, or Review again later
  8. 8The real-world action and its outcome are confirmed before any saving is counted

Recurr does not automatically cancel subscriptions, negotiate contracts, or guarantee savings. Choosing Cancel records a decision, not a completed cancellation. Potential savings are not realised savings — they only count as Verified savings once the real-world outcome is confirmed.
