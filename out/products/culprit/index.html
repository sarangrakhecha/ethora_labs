Culprit

Culprit is our proprietary Chrome extension for software debugging. It records what happened in the browser, ties each failed request back to the click that caused it, and turns that into a selector, a reproduction and a bug report you can paste straight into a ticket.

Available on the Chrome Web Store

Culprit is published and installable today.

Add to Chrome

  • Every failure leaves a clue. No accounts, nothing leaves your device.

  • One recommended selector, stated honestly — with the alternatives and their trade-offs shown alongside it.

  • Sensitive values are masked where recognised — applied before anything is shown or copied.

  • From click to bug report in one press: reproduction steps, the request, and the result, ready to paste into a ticket.

  • Capture, diagnosis and selector, end to end.

What it does

  • Failure capture, tied to a cause

    Failed requests are linked to the click that triggered them — “POST /api/v1/orders → 500, after Place order” — with a match confidence shown, not assumed.

  • Selectors you can trust

    For any element, Culprit suggests a selector rated unique, stable, or fragile — with the alternatives shown alongside it, copyable in your framework's syntax.

  • Network diagnostics

    Each captured request opens into overview, request, response and console views, with headers, payload and a ready-made cURL command.

  • Reproduction steps and screenshots

    The sequence leading to a failure becomes numbered reproduction steps in a copyable bug report, with a screenshot of the moment it broke.

  • Sensitive values masked

    Passwords, tokens, cards and keys are replaced with [REDACTED] where recognised, so a pasted bug report doesn't carry secrets.

  • Off by default, site by site

    Recording stays off until turned on for a given site, and runs locally — as a side panel or toolbar popup, in light and dark.
