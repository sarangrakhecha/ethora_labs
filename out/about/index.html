About

We design, build, test, and ship software.

Ethora Labs is an engineering-led technology consultancy, working across software engineering, quality engineering, test automation and CI/CD, and engineering prototypes — and leading with clear thinking and honest trade-offs rather than slogans.

The roots of our name

Our philosophy

Ethora comes from two Greek-rooted ideas: ethics (how we choose to work) and ethos (the character behind that choice) — in practice, that means treating most software problems as problems of judgement: what to build, what to verify, what to leave alone, and when to say an approach is not working.

So we lead with engineering judgement rather than with tools. We would rather spend a day understanding why a system behaves the way it does than a week working around it. We would rather tell you that a rewrite is not justified than sell you one — including when that is inconvenient.

  • Clear thinking

    Understand the real problem before proposing the answer.

  • Engineering rigour

    Build for maintainability, quality and considered trade-offs.

  • Honest assessment

    Make uncertainty and risk visible, even when inconvenient.

The long-term ambition is to build technology that matters — our own products alongside our consulting work — and to be the kind of company engineers want to work with twice.

See how that works in practice in our Design, Build, Verify, Improve cycle.

ETHORA, as a practice

Six behaviours, not six slogans — each one spelling out our own name, and each one something you could actually hold us to.

See how it shows up in our process
  • E

    Excellence

    We build verification into development rather than bolting it on before a release.

  • T

    Trust

    We report engineering risks honestly, including when the news is unwelcome or reflects badly on our own estimate.

  • H

    Humility

    We question assumptions, including our own, and investigate alternatives before committing.

  • O

    Ownership

    We build maintainable systems and write decisions down, so the work outlives our involvement.

  • R

    Rigour

    We understand the problem before choosing a solution, and we are willing to say the problem is not what you thought.

  • A

    Adaptability

    We let what verification and real use reveal shape what we build next, rather than treating a release as a finish line.

How we work with clients

We treat a working relationship as a long one, even before it starts. That means answering honestly — including when we are not the right fit, or when you may not need us at all — and proposing a first step small enough for you to judge the approach on before committing to anything larger.

01

We read it properly

A person reads your message. There is no sales pipeline here.

02

We reply honestly

Including when the answer is that we are not the right fit, or that you may not need us at all.

03

We propose something small

If it makes sense to work together, we suggest a first step small enough to judge us on.
